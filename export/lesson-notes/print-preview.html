Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students should be able to:

  1. Define chemical reactions with examples.
  2. Differentiate between exothermic and endothermic reactions.

Instructional Materials

Beakers, Bunsen burner, Magnesium ribbon, Test tubes, Periodic Table chart.

Step Teacher Activities Student Activities
