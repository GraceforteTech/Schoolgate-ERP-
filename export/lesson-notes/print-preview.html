Topic: Simultaneous Equations

Introduction to the concept of two variables and the elimination method for solving linear equations.

Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students should be able to identify simultaneous equations and solve them using the substitution method.

Teaching Procedure

1. Introduction: Review single variable equations.
2. Presentation: Introduce x and y variables.
3. Evaluation: Solve 3 sample problems on the board.
